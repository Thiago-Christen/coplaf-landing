# Floresta e serra — aprimoramento

Editada com ImageGen built-in a partir da fotografia enviada pelo usuário. Objetivo: melhorar resolução, definição da vegetação e cores naturais, mantendo a composição aproximada original, sem solicitar expansão do campo de visão ou conversão para 16:9. O original foi preservado.

Arquivo final: `floresta-serra-resolucao-melhorada.png`. Como há reconstrução generativa de detalhes finos, eles podem variar em relação ao original; a imagem não deve ser usada como registro documental exato. A resolução efetiva é a do PNG gerado, não necessariamente a dimensão ideal solicitada no prompt.

Edit target: the attached photograph of lush subtropical rainforest canopy framed by foreground trees and palms, distant blue-green mountain ridges, a pale blue sky with small white clouds. Improve ONLY image quality and resolution, with restrained natural color enhancement. Keep the original approximately 3:2 landscape aspect ratio, exact framing, camera viewpoint, tree silhouettes, palm fronds, canopy contours, distinctive mountain ridge shapes and original cloud positions. Increase resolution substantially, ideally around 3000 pixels wide or greater, with clean realistic fine foliage detail and reduced JPEG/compression artifacts. Enhance separation of leaf tones, gentle microcontrast and clarity; reveal a little detail in dark foliage without flattening original shadows. Maintain believable vibrant natural greens, the original sunny daylight, blue sky and distant atmospheric depth. Preserve the bright sky and sunlight direction. This must remain the SAME location and composition, not a new generated jungle. No crop, no 16:9 conversion, no outpainting, no wider FOV, no relocated mountain peaks, no new waterfalls, rivers, paths, buildings, animals or people, no added mist or dramatic sun rays, no sky replacement, no invented structures, no text or logos. No neon green saturation, excessive HDR, sharpening halos, plastic foliage, oil-painting effect or cinematic recoloring. One clean high-resolution photorealistic enhanced version of the supplied photograph.
